Why small businesses struggle with reporting

Reporting becomes difficult when information is spread across different files, definitions change between reports, or updates depend on one person. Start by choosing the decisions you need to make, agreeing on a few measures, and creating a repeatable monthly routine.

Five KPIs every business owner should track

A useful starting set might include revenue, gross margin, cash runway or cash flow, customer retention, and delivery capacity. The right measures depend on your business model; define each KPI clearly and review it at a cadence that supports decisions.

Data-driven decisions without expensive software

Before buying a new platform, review the capabilities of tools you already pay for. A well-structured spreadsheet, a clear source-of-truth, a few automated steps and a consistent review routine can be enough to make meaningful progress.

Turning spreadsheets into business intelligence

Use clear column names, one record per row, consistent data types, controlled categories and a separate summary view. Document key definitions, validate inputs, and avoid manually retyping figures between files wherever possible.
